Program Orion - Enterprise Growth Initiative

Section 1: Objectives
Program Orion aims to grow enterprise ARR by 18% between Q2 and Q4 of fiscal year 2026 through pricing optimization, partner-led distribution, and onboarding improvements.

Section 2: Financial Projections
Projected margin by quarter:
- Q2: 31%
- Q3: 34%
- Q4: 37%
Q4 is expected to lead due to lower discounting and improved onboarding automation.

Section 3: Risks
Top risks include:
1) CRM migration delays that may interrupt lead routing.
2) Regulatory review delays for cross-border data transfer.
3) Partner enablement lag causing inconsistent implementation quality.

Section 4: Dependencies
Launch sequencing depends on completion of identity federation rollout. Without identity federation, partner portal access cannot be enabled globally.

Section 5: Timeline
Pilot starts in late Q2, scale begins in Q3, and global roll-out occurs in Q4.
